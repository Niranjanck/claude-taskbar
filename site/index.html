Know what every session is doing

Claude Taskbar sits above your other windows and keeps one short list of your Claude work, so nothing waits on you unnoticed.

Every session at a glance

Each Claude Code session shows its task title, your latest prompt, and what Claude is doing right now, such as editing a file or running tests.

Answer without switching

Allow or deny permission prompts, pick answers to Claude’s multiple-choice questions, and reply when Claude ends with a question, right from the panel.

On top, on every screen

The panel floats above your windows, shows on every Space and over full-screen apps, and follows your mouse to whichever display you’re using.

claude.ai chats too

Add the Chrome extension and your claude.ai chats join the list, so you know when a long reply has finished. You can reply from the panel as well.

A list that stays tidy

Remove a finished task with one click. It comes back by itself the next time you ask that session something new.

Out of the way when you want

Hide the panel with one button or Control-Option-C, and bring it back the same way. A new question from Claude brings it back for you.

Made for working with Claude in parallel

Several sessions at once

Work across a few repositories and see which session needs you, without cycling through terminal tabs to check.

Long tasks in the background

Start a refactor or a test run, move on to something else, and see the moment Claude finishes or stops to ask you something.

No more stalled approvals

A command waiting for approval in a hidden terminal holds up the whole task. The panel shows it right away, with Allow and Deny.

Up and running in a minute

No scripts to run. The app sets everything up the first time you open it.

  1. 1

    Download and install

    Open the disk image and drag Claude Taskbar into your Applications folder.

  2. 2

    Connect to Claude Code

    Open the app and click Connect to Claude Code. It adds its hooks to your Claude Code settings, after backing them up.

  3. 3

    Keep working

    Restart your Claude Code sessions. From then on, each one appears in the panel as you work.

You’ll need

  • macOS 11 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel
  • Claude Code
  • Apple’s Command Line Tools, for Python 3. Most developers have them, and the app offers to install them if not.
  • Google Chrome, only if you want to follow claude.ai chats

Opening it the first time

Claude Taskbar isn’t from the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm before it opens the first time.

macOS 15 and later

Open the app once and click Done. Then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.

macOS 14 and earlier

Right-click Claude Taskbar in Applications, choose Open, then click Open again.

You only need to do this once.

Questions

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your Mac. Session status is written to small files in ~/.claude-taskbar, and the Chrome extension talks only to a local bridge at 127.0.0.1.

What does it change in Claude Code?

It adds hooks to ~/.claude/settings.json and saves a backup first. Your other settings and hooks stay as they are, and Disconnect from Claude Code in the menu bar removes them again.

Can I still answer in the terminal?

Yes. Every question in the panel has Answer in terminal, and a question you leave unanswered goes back to the terminal after 10 minutes.

Does it work with the Claude desktop app or Cowork?

Not at the moment. They don’t offer a way for other apps to read their status. Claude Taskbar works with Claude Code and with claude.ai in Chrome.

How do I remove it?

Click the Claude Taskbar icon in the menu bar, choose Disconnect from Claude Code, then Quit, and delete the app from Applications.

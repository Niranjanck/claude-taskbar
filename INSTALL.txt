Claude Taskbar
Every Claude task, always on top.

An always-on-top panel for macOS that lists your Claude Code sessions and claude.ai
chats, and lets you answer Claude's questions (permissions, multiple choice, and
follow-up questions) without switching windows.


INSTALL

1. Drag "Claude Taskbar" into the Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications.
3. Click "Connect to Claude Code" in the welcome window.
4. Restart any Claude Code sessions that are already open.

You need Claude Code and Apple's Command Line Tools (most developers have them;
if not, Claude Taskbar offers to install them).


IF MACOS SAYS IT CAN'T OPEN THE APP

This build isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time:

- macOS 15 or later: open the app once, click Done, then go to
  System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway".
- macOS 14 or earlier: right-click the app in Applications, choose Open,
  then click Open again.


CLAUDE.AI CHATS (OPTIONAL)

Click the Claude Taskbar icon in the menu bar and choose "Set Up Chrome Extension…".
Then follow the three steps it shows.


USING IT

- The panel floats above other windows and follows your mouse between screens.
- Click a task to jump to its terminal or Chrome tab.
- Hover over a task and click the cross to remove it from the list. It comes back
  when you ask that task something new.
- Drag the panel's header to move it; click it to show or hide the task list.
- To hide the panel, click the round button at the right of its header. Bring it back
  with Control-Option-C, the menu bar icon, or by opening Claude Taskbar again.
- Everything else is in the menu bar icon.


REMOVING IT

1. In the menu bar icon, choose "Disconnect from Claude Code", then Quit.
2. Delete Claude Taskbar from Applications.
3. Optionally, delete the folder ~/.claude-taskbar
